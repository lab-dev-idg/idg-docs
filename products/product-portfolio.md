# 2. پێوەرەکانی پەسەندکردنی بەرهەمی نوێ — New Product Evaluation Gates

Before any new initiative (`Product 002+`) is funded or codified into the official portfolio, it must pass five mandatory architectural gates:
1. **Strategic Coherence**: Direct alignment with IDG Vision 2030 and sovereign technological advancement.
2. **Platform Reusability**: Demonstrated leverage of existing IDG core platforms (IAM, Design System, Data Mesh).
3. **Trilingual Feasibility**: Native architectural support for Kurdish Sorani, Arabic, and English.
4. **Security & Sovereignty Model**: Verified cryptographic isolation and data residency compliance.
5. **Economic Viability**: Transparent value creation and sustainable operating model.

# 3. بەڕێوەبردنی کێبڕکێی نێوخۆیی — Anti-Cannibalization & Synergies

- Portfolio products must complement rather than duplicate existing product capabilities.
- Shared capabilities are migrated upstream into Tier 1 IDG Platform Services to prevent redundant engineering.
