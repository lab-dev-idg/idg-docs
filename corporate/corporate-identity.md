# 1. مەبەست و سەروەری دامەزراوە — Purpose & Master Brand Hierarchy

This specification defines the formal corporate and institutional identity standards for Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG). It strictly establishes the legal and branding hierarchy governing IDG as the holding parent entity and its relationship to present and future product assets.

# 3. جیاکردنەوەی براندی دامەزراوە بەرامبەر بەرهەم — Corporate vs. Product Identity

- **Corporate Communications (`IDG`)**:
  - Focus: Institutional authority, corporate governance, investor relations, sovereign technological capability, strategic partnerships.
  - Channels: `idg.global`, corporate whitepapers, legal contracts, board charters.
- **Product Communications (`AI Gate Iraq`)**:
  - Focus: Solution features, AI model access, developer APIs, documentation, product onboarding, enterprise workflows.
  - Channels: `aigate.iq`, product application portals, API developer hubs.

# 4. ڕێساکانی ناونانی بەرهەمە نوێیەکان — Naming Governance for Future Products

All future products (`Product 002+`) developed under IDG must adhere to the following naming architecture:
1. **Descriptive & Sovereign Alignment**: Names must clearly communicate functional purpose while retaining regional relevance and global clarity.
2. **Parent Endorsement**: When presented in institutional contexts, product branding must include the standard parent endorsement: `A Product of Iraq Digital Gateway` (یان `بەرهەمێکی دەروازەی دیجیتاڵی عێراق`).
3. **Domain Convention**: Products targeting sovereign or national end-users adopt the `.iq` ccTLD or designated IDG sub-domain conventions conforming to `IDG-SPEC-SEO-2026-V1`.
