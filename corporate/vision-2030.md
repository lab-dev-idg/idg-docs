# 1. مەبەست و ئاسۆی ستراتیژی — Purpose & Horizon Intent

**IDG Vision 2030** defines the long-term institutional roadmap, digital infrastructure ambitions, AI ecosystem expansion, and societal impact goals for Iraq Digital Gateway. 

Rather than relying on ungrounded financial projections, this document articulates the enduring technological and architectural trajectory governing IDG's multi-year evolution.

# 3. قۆناغەکانی گەیشتن بە دیدگای 2030 — Strategic Horizon Milestones

### قۆناغی 1: چەسپاندنی پلاتفۆرمی بنەڕەتی — Phase 1: Core Foundation & Platform Supremacy
- Hardening of parent enterprise infrastructure (`idg.global`).
- Production scale deployment of Product 001 (`AI Gate Iraq` / `aigate.iq`).
- Full implementation of Tier 1 Enterprise Architecture Specifications.

### قۆناغی 2: فرەیی بەرهەم و خزمەتگوزارییەکان — Phase 2: Portfolio Expansion & Vertical Integration
- Incubation and launch of vertical platforms (`Product 002+`) for government, finance, and enterprise workflow modernization.
- Expansion of developer ecosystem and API marketplace across the region.

### قۆناغی 3: سەروەری دیجیتاڵی تەواو — Phase 3: Sovereign Ecosystem Maturity
- Autonomous, self-healing multi-cloud infrastructure operating across domestic data centers.
- End-to-end trilingual knowledge graph serving as the definitive cognitive backbone for enterprise operations.
