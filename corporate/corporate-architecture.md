# 1. مەبەست و مەودا — Purpose & Scope

This specification establishes the master enterprise architecture framework for Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG). It defines the structural synthesis uniting corporate governance, business architecture, digital capability models, technology platforms, and portfolio assets into an integrated institutional ecosystem.

### مەودای جێبەجێکردن — Scope of Application:
- **Parent Holding Authority**: Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG) as the apex corporate entity.
- **Product Portfolio**: AI Gate Iraq (Product 001) and all future products (`Product 002` through `Product 500+`).
- **Operational Layers**: Corporate governance, shared enterprise services, technical platforms, data assets, and digital infrastructure.

---

# 2. بنەما دەستوورییەکانی تەلارسازی — Constitutional Architecture Principles

1. **سەروەری دامەزراوەی دایک — Parent Entity Supremacy**: IDG is the sovereign institutional holding enterprise. All subsidiaries, specialized platforms, and product lines derive their mandate and architectural compliance from IDG.
2. **سەربەخۆیی بەڕێوەبردنی بەرهەم — Product Autonomy within Guardrails**: Individual product lines operate autonomously in their market execution while strictly adhering to enterprise data, security, design, and API standards.
3. **تەلارسازی یەکگرتووی دیجیتاڵی — Digital-First Architecture**: Every corporate capability must be designed for digital execution, API interoperability, automation, and machine readability.
4. **سەروەری و پاراستنی داتا — Sovereign Data & Cryptographic Integrity**: All enterprise data assets adhere to sovereign hosting, strict Zero Trust boundaries, and auditable lineage.
5. **یەکسانی سێ زمانە — Trilingual Parity by Design**: Corporate governance, communication, and systems support English (`en-US`), Arabic (`ar-IQ`), and Kurdish Sorani (`ckb-IQ`).

# 4. یەکگرتوویی ستراتیژی و تەلارسازی — Strategic Alignment Model

To ensure zero drift between executive vision and execution, all initiatives must validate against three strategic pillars:
- **National Digital Enablement**: Providing scalable digital infrastructure that powers enterprise and public modernization.
- **AI & Intelligent Systems**: Delivering sovereign, production-grade intelligence infrastructure anchored by Product 001 (`AI Gate Iraq`).
- **Sustainable Institutional Value**: Building resilient platforms capable of scaling to hundreds of product iterations over a multi-decade lifecycle.
