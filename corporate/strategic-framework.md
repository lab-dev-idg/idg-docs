# 1. ئەرک و دیدگا — Mission & Vision

### پەیام و ئەرک — Enterprise Mission:
To engineer, operate, and govern sovereign, production-grade digital infrastructure, enterprise platforms, and artificial intelligence gateways that accelerate national digital transformation, foster economic resilience, and empower modern institutional workflows across Iraq and the wider region.

### دیدگا — Enterprise Vision:
To be the definitive institutional digital gateway and technological foundation for the nation, setting the benchmark for sovereign AI orchestration, digital infrastructure excellence, and multilingual accessibility by 2030.

# 3. ئامانجە ستراتیژییەکان و پێوانەکردن — Strategic Objectives & Measurement

1. **Infrastructure Reliability**: Achieve and sustain >= 99.95% system uptime across all public and enterprise services.
2. **API & Developer Adoption**: Maintain zero-breaking-change backwards compatibility across all published major API versions.
3. **Data Protection & Compliance**: Maintain 100% cryptographic enforcement on sensitive data at rest and in transit with zero plaintext leakage.
4. **Localization Parity**: Maintain 100% translation completeness across Kurdish Sorani, Arabic, and English across all public surfaces.

# 4. شێوازی جێبەجێکردنی ستراتیژی — Strategic Execution & Review Cycle

- **Planning Horizons**: Strategic planning operates on a 3-tier horizon model:
  - **Horizon 1 (Current Quarter / Year)**: Operational execution and core platform hardening.
  - **Horizon 2 (1-3 Years)**: Portfolio expansion (`Product 002+`) and enterprise integration.
  - **Horizon 3 (3-5+ Years / Vision 2030)**: Sovereign ecosystem supremacy and advanced research.
- **Executive Review**: The Executive Council convenes quarterly to evaluate objective progression and calibrate resource allocation.
