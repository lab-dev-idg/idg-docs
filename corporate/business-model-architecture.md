# 2. پەیوەندی و پێکهاتەی سێ ئاستی بازرگانی — Three-Tier Product & Platform Hierarchy

IDG structures its commercial and business operations across three distinct tiers:

### ئاستی 1: پلاتفۆرمی بنەڕەتی دامەزراوە — Tier 1: Enterprise Platform Core
- **خاوەندارێتی — Ownership**: Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG Core).
- **مەودا — Scope**: Identity federation (OIDC), global payment/billing gateways, trilingual translation engines, Zero Trust security fabric, and shared cloud hosting.
- **مۆدێل — Model**: Internal shared services providing foundation for all product lines.

### ئاستی 2: بەرهەمە سەرەکییەکان — Tier 2: Flagship Product Offerings (Product 001: AI Gate Iraq)
- **خاوەندارێتی — Ownership**: Product Units under IDG umbrella.
- **مەودا — Scope**: Specialized domain products addressing high-value enterprise and public needs (e.g. AI Gate Iraq providing national-scale LLM orchestration, model hosting, knowledge retrieval, and trilingual AI gateways).
- **مۆدێل — Model**: Tiered SaaS, capacity licensing, enterprise API invocation quotas, and customized institutional deployment enclaves.

### ئاستی 3: بەرهەم و خزمەتگوزارییە پەیوەستکراوەکان — Tier 3: Connected Portfolio Ecosystem (`Product 002+`)
- **مەودا — Scope**: Future vertical platforms (Government Solutions, Enterprise Workflow Automation, National Knowledge Graphs).
- **مۆدێل — Model**: Plug-and-play modular services leveraging established Tier 1 and Tier 2 architecture.

# 3. مۆدێلی ژینگەی هاوبەش و بەشداربووان — Platform Ecosystem Model

The IDG business model is built around a multi-sided ecosystem connecting four critical stakeholder groups:
1. **Enterprise & Institutional Clients**: Organizations consuming reliable, secure, localized digital and AI solutions.
2. **Developers & Integrators**: Technical teams leveraging OpenAPI 3.1 contracts, SDKs, and developer portals to build integrated workflows.
3. **Technology & Infrastructure Partners**: Cloud providers, specialized compute vendors, and telecommunications networks ensuring low-latency delivery.
4. **Public & Community Users**: End-users interacting with seamless, localized, trilingual digital interfaces.

# 4. گەشەسەندنی مۆدێلی کار بەپێی کات — Business Model Evolution & Governance

- **قۆناغی دامەزراندن و پلاتفۆرمی بنەڕەتی — Phase 1: Foundation & Core Platforms**: Solidify IDG infrastructure, establish Product 001 (`AI Gate Iraq`), and achieve high availability.
- **قۆناغی گەشەکردن و فرەبەرهەمی — Phase 2: Portfolio Expansion**: Deploy standardized product templates to launch subsequent vertical assets (`Product 002+`) at zero foundational marginal cost.
- **قۆناغی سەروەری و سەرانسەری — Phase 3: Sovereign Ecosystem Supremacy**: Establish IDG as the benchmark digital ecosystem provider across regional and global markets.
