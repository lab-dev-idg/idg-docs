# 2. ڕێساکانی بەکارهێنانی وێنە — Usage & Composition Rules

- **Zero Generic Clichés**: Strictly prohibit stock images of glowing handshake silhouettes, artificial blue brain circuits, floating holographic globes, or generic business suits.
- **Aspect Ratios**: Standardized to `16:9` for horizontal hero headers, `4:3` for article thumbnails, and `1:1` for profile/author avatars.
- **Optimization Standards**: All web imagery must be encoded in WebP or AVIF format with explicit `width`, `height`, responsive `srcset`, and localized `alt` tags.
