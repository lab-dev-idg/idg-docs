# 2. ڕێساکانی تێکەڵنەکردن و بەکارهێنانی هەڵە — Prohibited Misuse Rules

To preserve brand integrity, the following alterations are strictly forbidden:
- **No Distortion**: Never stretch, condense, skew, or rotate the logo lockup.
- **No Color Alteration**: Do not substitute approved brand colors with unapproved tints or gradients.
- **No Low Contrast Placement**: Never place the full-color dark logo over complex imagery or dark navy backgrounds.
- **No Element Separation**: Never detach the symbol mark from the logotype without explicit authorization for standalone favicons/app icons.

# 3. پەیوەندی لۆگۆی دامەزراوە و لۆگۆی بەرهەمەکان — Product Endorsement Lockup

When displaying Product 001 (`AI Gate Iraq`), the endorsement line must maintain optical alignment with the primary mark:
- Proportion: Product logo occupies `100%` scale; Parent endorsement occupies `40%` scale.
- Placement: Bottom or right-aligned depending on layout direction (LTR vs RTL).
