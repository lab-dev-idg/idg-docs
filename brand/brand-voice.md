# 1. بنەماکانی دەنگی براند — Core Voice Principles

The communication voice of Iraq Digital Gateway across English, Kurdish Sorani, and Arabic reflects institutional authority, technical rigor, and sovereign trust.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. دەسەڵاتدار و وردبین — AUTHORITATIVE & PRECISE                             │
│ Fact-driven, unambiguous statements; zero colloquial or hype language      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. بونیاتنەر و سەروەر — INSTITUTIONAL & SOVEREIGN                           │
│ Focused on long-term national digital value, stability, and enduring trust  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. ئەندازیاری و ڕوون — CLEAR & SYSTEMATIC                                   │
│ Direct explanations, deterministic definitions, and structured logic        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. ڕێزدار و فرەزمان — INCLUSIVE & RESPECTFUL                                │
│ Natural, culturally dignified phrasing in Kurdish Sorani, Arabic, and English│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

# 3. وشە و دەستەواژە قەدەغەکراوەکان — Prohibited Language & Expressions

To protect institutional credibility, the following language patterns are strictly prohibited across all IDG content:
- **No Hyperbolic Startup Slang**: Do not use "supercharge", "revolutionary", "magic", "game-changing", "ninja", or "rockstar".
- **No Unverifiable Marketing Claims**: Avoid "the best in the world", "unmatched speed", or "limitless potential".
- **No Awkward Machine Translation**: Kurdish Sorani and Arabic texts must be authored with professional native fluency rather than transliterated English syntax.
