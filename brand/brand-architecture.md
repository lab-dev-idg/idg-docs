# 1. مەبەست و مۆدێلی براندی دامەزراوە — Purpose & Master Brand Model

This specification establishes the structural relationship between Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG) as the parent corporate master brand and its specialized product lines (starting with Product 001: `AI Gate Iraq`).

IDG employs an **Endorsed Brand Architecture (مۆدێلی براندی پەسەندکراو)**:

                      ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐

# 2. چوار ئاستی دەربڕینی براند — Four Levels of Brand Expression

1. **Corporate Master Brand (`IDG`)**:
   - Primary Focus: Trust, sovereignty, national digital foundation, enterprise stability.
   - Applications: Legal agreements, institutional website (`idg.global`), investor materials, governance charters.
2. **Flagship Product Brand (`AI Gate Iraq`)**:
   - Primary Focus: Advanced intelligence, speed, developer productivity, enterprise AI capabilities.
   - Applications: Product portal (`aigate.iq`), web consoles, developer documentation, API hubs.
3. **Digital Interface Brand**:
   - Primary Focus: Usability, low cognitive load, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), high contrast, deterministic response.
   - Applications: UI components, input forms, data tables, navigation bars.
4. **Corporate Communications**:
   - Primary Focus: Transparency, technical accuracy, sovereign digital transformation.
   - Applications: Whitepapers, architecture decision records, engineering changelogs.

# 3. ڕێساکانی براندسازی هاوبەش — Co-Branding & Endorsement Guidelines

- **Product Endorsement Lockup**: When displaying product branding in institutional or marketing environments, the product logo or title must be accompanied by the standard endorsement mark:
  - English: `AI Gate Iraq — An Iraq Digital Gateway Product`
  - Kurdish Sorani: `دەروازەی ژیریی عێراق — بەرهەمێکی دەروازەی دیجیتاڵی عێراق`
  - Arabic: `بوابة الذكاء الاصطناعي العراق — أحد منتجات بوابة العراق الرقمية`
- **Visual Independence**: Products utilize the shared IDG design system tokens, ensuring visual family resemblance while retaining distinctive product-tier accentuation.
