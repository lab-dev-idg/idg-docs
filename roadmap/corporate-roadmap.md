# 1. مەبەست و کایەی دامەزراوەیی — Purpose & Corporate Evolution

This roadmap establishes the progressive maturity of corporate governance, organizational structures, institutional partnerships, and regulatory alignments across IDG's strategic horizons.

---

# 2. قۆناغەکانی گەشەی دامەزراوەیی — Corporate Evolutionary Horizons

### ئاسۆی 1: چوارچێوەی دەستووری و بەڕێوەبردن — Horizon 1: Governance & Institutional Grounding
- Formalize all Tier 0 and Tier 1 Enterprise Architecture Specifications.
- Establish the Enterprise Architecture Council (EAC) and Security & Sovereignty Board.
- Deploy trilingual corporate portal (`idg.global`) and authoritative documentation repository.

### ئاسۆی 2: هاوبەشی ستراتیژی و گەشەی هەرێمی — Horizon 2: Strategic Partnerships & Scale
- Establish bilateral technology integration frameworks with enterprise and sovereign institutions.
- Standardize multi-tenant enterprise contracts, SLA guarantees, and institutional support models.
- Operationalize annual corporate maturity audits and DACI review cycles.

### ئاسۆی 3: پەیمانگای نیشتمانی و سەروەری گشتی — Horizon 3: Institutional Supremacy (Vision 2030)
- Recognized as the definitive national sovereign digital infrastructure provider.
- Full operational autonomy across domestic infrastructure, sovereign compute, and regional technology alliances.

---

# 3. حوکمڕانی و کۆنتڕۆڵی نەخشەڕێگا — Corporate Review Cadence

The Executive Council audits corporate roadmap progression semi-annually, validating institutional milestones against Vision 2030 strategic pillars.
